We welcome enquiries from across Kenya. Share your town or neighbourhood so the team can confirm availability for your area.

How is my quote worked out?+

Quotes are discussed around the service, the size and condition of the space, your location and the details you share. We do not publish a one price fits all rate.

What can I include in my request?+

Tell us the type of space, which rooms need attention, your preferred timing and any particular priorities. The team can confirm the scope with you.

Does the form book a clean automatically?+

No. It prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Your visit is only arranged after you discuss the details with Sahlan.
